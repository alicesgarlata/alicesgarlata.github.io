About Alice

Writing, designing
and building with curiosity.

I’m Alice, a multidisciplinary creative working across editorial writing, visual communication and digital humanities.

My practice moves between music journalism, graphic design, cultural communication and interactive web projects. I write about independent music and cultural scenes, create visual communication for concerts and events in Bologna, and build digital projects around cultural heritage, archives and storytelling.

I’m interested in the point where content, design and technology meet: how research becomes a story, how information becomes an interface, and how digital tools can make culture more accessible and engaging.

Puntolineare is the nickname I use for my personal illustration practice — a space for experimentation, visual notes and things made simply because they spark curiosity.
