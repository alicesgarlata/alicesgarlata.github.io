Hi, I’m Alice · Bologna, Italy

Writing, designing
and building with
curiosity.

I’m a multidisciplinary creative and Digital Humanities student moving between editorial writing, visual communication and interactive digital projects.

I’m interested in independent culture, live music, cultural heritage and the small details that give places and communities their identity. I like learning by making things, and most of my work starts with research, curiosity and a question I want to follow.

01

Music

New sounds, small venues and independent scenes often become the starting point for my writing and design work.

02

Drawing

Plants, places and everyday details feed my personal illustration practice, Puntolineare.

03

Archives

I like understanding how cultural material is organised, preserved and turned into something people can explore.

04

Learning

I research, test, fail, adjust and try again. Curiosity is usually the thread connecting everything.

What I do

I WRITE

Music journalism, cultural commentary, interviews and editorial content.

01

I write to make ideas clear, engaging and worth following.

From album reviews to cultural features, I like finding an angle, doing the research and building a voice around it.

02

I design to give ideas a visual language.

I work with colour, typography, illustration and layout to create communication that feels connected to the people and places behind it.

03

I build when a story needs to become an experience.

Maps, knowledge graphs, interfaces and small digital experiments let me combine research, structure and interaction.
